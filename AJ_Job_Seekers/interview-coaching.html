Before you start

Get the best from your practice interview.

Use a supported browser

Voice answers work best in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer or Android phone. In other browsers you can type your answers instead.

Allow the microphone

Your browser will ask for permission to use the microphone. Chrome and Edge send speech to their own speech service to turn it into text. AJ Job Seekers does not record or store your voice.

Find a quiet place

Use headphones if you can, so the interviewer’s voice is not picked up as part of your answer. Speak clearly at a steady pace.

Understand the score

Scores are automatic and based on structure, examples, relevance, length and delivery of what you said. They are a practice guide, not a promise about a real interview.
