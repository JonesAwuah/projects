FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I post a job on AJ Job Seekers?
Choose “Employer: post a job” in the form above and fill in the company name, job title, number of positions and the application due date. We review the details and publish the vacancy for you on the Jobs page.
Is it free for job seekers?
Yes. Searching vacancies, the coaching tools and the guides are free for job seekers. We will never ask you for money to apply for a job.
How long will you take to reply?
We aim to reply within one to two working days. Messages sent on weekends or public holidays are answered on the next working day.
Someone asked me to pay for a job. Is that from you?
No. AJ Job Seekers never charges applicants, and real employers do not either. Please do not pay, and report the message to us using “Feedback or report a problem” so that we can warn others.
Can you edit or remove a vacancy I posted?
Yes. Send us the company name and job title with the change you want, and we will update or remove the vacancy.
I need help with my CV or interview. Where do I start?
Try the Resume builder, the Interview Coaching and the Career Guides. If you are still stuck, choose “Job seeker support” and tell us what you need.
